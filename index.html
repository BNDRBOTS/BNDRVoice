How it works

From writing sample to voice blueprint in four steps

No training runs. No uploads to our servers. Your sample goes straight from your browser to your own AI provider and comes back decoded.

STEP 01

Paste your writing

An email, a post, a chapter — anything you actually wrote. 150–600 words is the sweet spot.

STEP 02

Get decoded

The engine dissects tone, rhythm, structure, vocabulary, and risk posture across 16 dimensions — with scores you can inspect and correct.

STEP 03

Set the rules

Pick intent, audience, and content type. Flip on hard filters like NO HYPE, NO HEDGING, NO AI TELLS — enforced, not suggested.

STEP 04

Deploy anywhere

Copy the machine file into ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI tool as a system prompt. Keep the human doc for your team.

FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers

Do I need my own AI key?
Yes. VoiceEngine drives your own Anthropic, OpenAI, or DeepSeek account. That's why it's cheap, and why we never see your writing. Keys take about 2 minutes to create and the app walks you through it.
Which AI tools can use my voice profile?
Any tool that accepts a system prompt or custom instructions: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, API integrations, agents — the machine file is plain JSON with an embedded system prompt.
Is my writing stored anywhere?
Your sample goes from your browser to your AI provider and back. We store only the finished voice profiles you explicitly save — and lifetime users can keep even those on-device only.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Anytime, in one click, from the account menu inside the app. You keep access until the end of the paid period. No emails, no phone calls, no retention scripts.
How does the lifetime license work?
You buy once on Gumroad and get a license key. Paste it into the app's "Redeem access" screen — it verifies with Gumroad and unlocks unlimited use on that device, no account needed.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes — the whole flow is mobile-first: analysis, filters, exports, saving, and the guided tour all work on any modern phone browser.
